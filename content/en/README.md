# Introduction

[MetaMystia](https://github.com/MetaMystia/MetaMystia) is an unofficial mod for the game [Touhou Mystia's Izakaya](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1584090/). Its main features are multiplayer and the ResourceEx content expansion.

If you are not familiar with the word "mod", you can think of it as:

> A patch-style plugin installed on top of the original game, adding features the game did not have before.

Its goal is simple:

> To make Touhou Mystia's Izakaya, once single-player only, truly lively.

With MetaMystia, you and your friends can gather ingredients on your own during the day and run the izakaya together at night. You can also install ResourceEx resource packs to experience new characters, dishes, and stories.

## Multiplayer

The original Touhou Mystia's Izakaya has no multiplayer support. After installing MetaMystia, one player can open a room and others can join it, working through the daytime activities and the night shift together.

You can play together through the test play server, or connect directly within a LAN.

Rooms hold up to 2 players by default, and the limit can be adjusted when opening the room to play with three or more.

While playing together:

- During the day you can see other players; everyone still uses their own save file and does daytime activities on their own;
- Once every player has finished the day, you choose a shop together;
- Before opening, each player can choose a menu, beverages, and cookware; the shift starts after all players confirm;
- During the shift you can split the work: cooking, picking up ingredients, serving dishes, and greeting guests;
- In game you can view the player list, send text messages, and switch character appearances;
- The host can change the player limit or remove a specific player.

## Skin system

The skin system lets players use characters and outfits from the base game, DLC, and ResourceEx, as well as online skins downloaded from skin sites.

Each player can choose a different appearance, and the choice is synced to everyone else in the room. See [Skin system](./user_guide/skin.md) for details.

## ResourceEx content expansion

ResourceEx is the content expansion feature of MetaMystia. It can add to the game:

- New special guests and portraits;
- New ingredients, dishes, and beverages;
- New dialogue, bond quests, and events;
- New merchants, outfits, and items;
- Resource pack gift mailbox and character spell cards.

Regular players only need to install resource packs made by others. Creators who want to make their own content can see [ResourceEx content expansion](./resource_ex/use_resource-ex.md).

### Example resource pack

`MetaMystia-ResourceExample` is the example resource pack provided by the project. It adds characters such as Daiyousei, Koakuma, Flandre, Eirin, Shinki, the Aki sisters, Ran, Yuki, and Mai.

The example pack also includes custom ingredients, dishes, and beverages, plus dialogue, bond quests, merchants, and outfits for the new characters. Some character stories are still in production; the contents of the currently released pack take precedence.

## Current limitations

MetaMystia is still under development. For now, note that:

- All players should use the same game version and MetaMystia version;
- When playing ResourceEx content together, all players should install the same version of the resource pack;
- Some stories, challenges, trials, and cooking duels are not fully adapted to multiplayer yet.

When you run into an unadapted story, you can disconnect from multiplayer, finish that day's flow on your own, and then reconnect to continue.
